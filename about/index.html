Our story

Built on a decade of lifts done right.

Wisejet Cranes has spent more than ten years helping contractors, mines, manufacturers and infrastructure owners move what others can't. Our fleet, topped by our 300 tonne crane, is one of the biggest in the country.

From our base on Robert Mugabe Road in Harare we mobilise anywhere in Zimbabwe, bringing the right machine, experienced operators and qualified riggers to every job, planned around your site.
