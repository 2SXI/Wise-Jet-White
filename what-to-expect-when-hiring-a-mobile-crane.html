What to Expect When You Hire a Mobile Crane

August 22, 2026  ·  4 min read  ·  By the Wisejet team

Hiring a crane for the first time can feel unfamiliar. The process is simple when you know the stages.

1. Enquiry and quotation

You describe the job: what is being lifted, where, and when. We recommend a suitable crane and crew and issue a quotation.

2. Planning

For anything beyond a routine lift, a plan is prepared covering the crane position, ground, rigging and exclusion zone. Clear information from you makes this faster.

3. Mobilisation

The crane and crew travel to your site. Allow for access, set-up time and any road or gate restrictions.

4. The lift

The operator and riggers carry out the lift to plan, with a briefing first. Your site team's main role is to keep the area clear and the ground ready.

5. Demobilisation

Once the work is complete, the crane is packed up and leaves site. Any follow-up lifts can be scheduled at this point.

Ready to start? Call us or send the quote form and we will guide you through each step.
