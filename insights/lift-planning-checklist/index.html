A Practical Lift Planning Checklist for Site Managers

10 September 2026 · 5 min read · By the Wisejet team

The best lifts are the boring ones. They are boring because the thinking happened beforehand. Use this checklist when preparing a lifting operation on your site.

Before the day

  • Confirm the load weight, dimensions and lifting points.
  • Agree pick-up and set-down positions and the travel path of the load.
  • Check access routes for the crane and delivery vehicles.
  • Identify overhead hazards such as power lines and structures.
  • Prepare the ground and exclusion zone.

On the day

  • Hold a briefing so operator, rigger and signaller know the plan.
  • Agree clear communication, hand signals or radios.
  • Keep unauthorised people out of the lifting zone.
  • Stop the lift if conditions such as wind change.
